## 2. Description of Service

BiteSwipe is a group restaurant discovery application that allows users to:
- Create or join groups with other users.
- Swipe yes or no on restaurant cards sourced from the Google Maps Places API.
- Receive "match" notifications when group members mutually swipe yes on the same restaurant.
- Chat with group members within the App.

The App is available in two tiers:
- **Free Tier:** Full core functionality with periodic **Sponsored** restaurant cards displayed between organic swipes.
- **Paid Tier (BiteSwipe Premium):** Ad-free experience via a monthly subscription.

We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any feature of the App at any time with reasonable notice.
